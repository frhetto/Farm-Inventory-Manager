Project Overview & Problem Statement

Solving fragmented agricultural records with a unified operational and financial ledger

The Core Problem

In the absence of a centralized system, the datasets for farm management are handled separately across disjointed spreadsheets, paper logs, and disparate tools. This leads to severe data inconsistencies, stock discrepancies, unaccounted equipment depreciation, and delayed financial visibility.

Data Asymmetry & Stock Discrepancies

Why is such a system required?

As the agricultural sector grows, it becomes increasingly difficult to keep track of operations across separated datasets. A centralized system handles inventory and operation records efficiently, establishing a transparent, consistent, and automated method to manage inventories regardless of farm acreage or team size.

Single Source of Operational Truth

Who needs this system?

Large crop farm owners, farm managers, agricultural field workers, contract farmers, and backyard growers who need disciplined oversight of their yields and inputs.

System Requirements Matrix

Comprehensive functional specifications categorized into 10 core system domains

REQ-01 Process 1.0

1. User & Role Management

Register new users with specified agricultural scales (smallholding, commercial crop farm, livestock ranch, greenhouse, garden). Assign granular roles (Superadmin/Developer, Owners, Managers, Workers, Gardeners) and manage secure user profiles.

REQ-02 Process 2.0

2. Inventory Management

Manage main categories (seeds, tools, livestock, fertilizers, produce). Automatically update stock upon purchasing or outgoing sales. Verify record deletions with audit trails, track stock movement history, define physical location hierarchies (barn, field, warehouse), and execute inter-location transfers.

REQ-03 Process 3.0

3. Operational Management

Record daily worker activities (feeding livestock, planting seeds, applying treatments). Directly link activities to automated inventory depletion. Schedule routine maintenance for agricultural tools and livestock health/vaccination cycles, with approval/rejection workflows by managers.

REQ-04 Process 4.0

4. Financial & Expense Management

Record operational expenses categorized by sections (purchasing, repairs, utilities, labor). Capture sales revenue transactions, categorize financial accounts, and automatically calculate total operational costs, gross income, and profit margins.

REQ-05 Process 5.0

5. Reporting & Visualization Management

Generate current stock summaries based on categories or items. Export reports into external formats (PDF, XLSX, CSV). Provide role-tailored visual dashboards (simple interfaces for workers; detailed analytical summaries with AI-assisted insights for owners/managers), and broadcast early low-stock warnings.

REQ-06 Process 6.0

6. Supplier Management

Maintain a comprehensive directory of suppliers, vendors, and distributors via API, XLSX, or CSV ingestion. Create and track purchase orders with safety fulfillment monitoring, and benchmark supplier price histories for cost optimization.

REQ-07 Process 7.0

7. Asset Maintenance Management

Schedule periodic maintenance and routine servicing for heavy equipment and machinery. Record repair history, maintenance costs, and asset physical conditions. Issue automated early warnings when service dates approach or warranties expire.

REQ-08 Process 8.0

8. Task Management

Create, read, update, delete (CRUD), and assign field tasks to designated workers. Track live task progress and statuses (pending, in-progress, blocked, completed), and validate task sign-offs upon completion.

REQ-09 Process 9.0

9. Offline & Synchronization Management

Store all inputs locally on the client device (offline-first architecture). Automatically synchronize pending local queue batches to the central server upon network reconnection, with robust conflict resolution rules for concurrent edits.

REQ-10 Process 10.0

10. External Integration Management

Expose secure REST APIs for external consumption. Ingest live external data feeds (weather forecasts, agronomic recommendations, market prices), and export financial/inventory records to third-party accounting and ERP systems.
